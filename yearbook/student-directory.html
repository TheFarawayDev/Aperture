Operators & shortcuts

Basics

Type part of a name, a room, a teacher or a grade. Typos are forgiven. The menus filter by grade, period and teacher without any syntax.

Operators

Combine clauses with commas, written as key: value.

teach: nameStudents with that teacher at any point.
activeper: 1-7Limit room/teacher searches to one period.
room: 201Students who have a class in that room.
lunch: AStudents on a lunch block (A, B or C).
grade: 11Students in a grade.

Example: marshall, activeper: 5, teach: phiri

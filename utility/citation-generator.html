How it works

This tool follows the published rules for each style: author order and initials, capitalization, italics, punctuation, dates, and what to do when a part is missing. There is no AI and no guessing. The same input always gives the same result.

  • Look-ups. A DOI or ISBN can fill in the details. This uses the free Crossref and Open Library services. Always check the names and titles it returns.
  • Your list is saved in this browser only. It is sorted alphabetically for you, and the Word file uses double spacing and a hanging indent.
  • Always check against your teacher's instructions. Some assignments ask for small changes to the style.

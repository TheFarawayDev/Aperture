Pick a nickname and a password. People find and message you by your nickname, so you never have to share your real name.

3 to 20 letters, numbers or underscores.
Write your password down somewhere safe. There is no email, so a lost password cannot be reset, and your old messages cannot be read without it.
All chats are monitored. Messages are encrypted, and site administrators can review any chat. Reported messages are always looked at. If anything bad happens while using Messages, Aperture and Faros Technologies Inc. are not responsible. Every message can be reported with the flag button.
Be safe and kind
  • Do not use your real name, school, address or phone number in your nickname or messages.
  • Only text is allowed. Links, emails and phone numbers are removed.
  • You can block anyone or report a message at any time.
  • Messages you send are deleted after 30 days. Only people 13 and older may use Messages.

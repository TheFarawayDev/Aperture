HOW TO PLAY

1. On the map, pick a county to restore. Its light then spreads to neighbouring counties. Win the USA first, then help Canada, Mexico, Alaska, Central America, South America, the islands and Greenland. Complete a whole continent to reach Europe.
1b. Press U for the UNION screen: sign compacts with factions, set each border open / checkpoint / closed, and keep your station links stable. Pick a difficulty when you start (change it any time in SETTINGS).
2. In a county, lay POWER, WATER and NET lines to every district hub. Left-drag to build, WASD or right-drag to look around.
3. Generators burn fuel, so build a refinery. Houses and workshops earn scrap; workshops also make parts for repairs.
4. Research new buildings (Y). Watch ALERTS: breakdowns, storms and fuel shortages will test you.
5. You can leave a county half-restored and come back. Restored counties can suffer outages: go back and repair them.
Counties autosave when you leave. Press ? in a county for every shortcut.

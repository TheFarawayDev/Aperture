      How to play

      1. You get one clue about a mystery character, starting vague.
      2. Search the list, pick your guess and press Guess.
      3. A wrong guess or a skip reveals the next clue. You have 5 chances.
      4. The fewer clues you need, the more points you earn.
      • If you pick the right group but the wrong answer, it tells you.
      • Hints are optional and cost a few points each.
      • Daily gives everyone the same answer, chosen from the date. Free play lets you filter by group.

      How to play

      1. Tap a square, then type. Tap it again to switch between across and down.
      2. Tap any clue to jump to its word.
      3. Arrow keys move around. Backspace deletes. Space switches direction.
      • Check marks wrong letters in red.
      • Reveal letter and Reveal word are there if you get stuck, but they count as help.
      • Every puzzle is built fresh, so no two are the same.

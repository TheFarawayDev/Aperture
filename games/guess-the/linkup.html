How to play

  1. Pick four words you think belong together, then press Submit.
  2. Right: the group is revealed with its name. Wrong: you lose one of four mistakes.
  3. If three of your four are right, it says you are one away.
  • Gold groups are the easiest, then green, blue and violet.
  • Violet groups are word play, like words that can follow the same word.
  • There is no daily limit. A new puzzle is built each time.

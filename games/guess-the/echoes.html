    How to play

    1. Press play to hear a very short clip of a song.
    2. Search for the song and pick it from the list, then press Guess.
    3. A wrong guess or a skip unlocks a longer clip. You have 5 chances.
    4. The fewer seconds you need, the more points you earn.
    • If you pick the right artist but the wrong song, it tells you.
    • Hints are optional. Each one costs a few points.
    • Daily gives everyone the same song, chosen from the date. Free play lets you pick a genre, era, difficulty, or an artist or Apple Music link.

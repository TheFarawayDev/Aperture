How to play

  1. Tap the scrambled letters, or type them, to spell the hidden word.
  2. Tap a filled box to take a letter back. Backspace removes the last letter.
  3. The word checks itself when every box is filled.
  • Hint shows the category first, then reveals letters, and costs points.
  • Faster answers earn a time bonus. Wrong answers break your streak only if you skip.
  • Any real word that uses all the letters counts.

How to play

  1. Guess the hidden five-letter word in six tries.
  2. Each guess must be a real word. Press Enter to submit.
  3. Tiles show how close you are.
  • Green: right letter, right spot.
  • Gold: right letter, wrong spot.
  • Dark: the letter is not in the word.
  • There is no daily limit. Press New word whenever you want another.

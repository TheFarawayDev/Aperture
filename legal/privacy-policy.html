Privacy Policy

Last updated October 25, 2026 · Aperture is owned and operated by Faros Technologies Inc.

Aperture is a set of free school tools. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. This page explains the small amount of information the tools do use.

The short version

  • You do not create an account, and Aperture does not ask for your name, email or password.
  • Settings you choose (such as your lunch or class periods) are saved in your own browser, on your own device.
  • Aperture does not sell personal information and does not show advertising.
  • A few tools contact outside services to work (weather, ZIP lookup, shared school calendar). They are listed below.

What stays on your device

Aperture uses your browser's local and session storage to remember things between visits. This can include your selected lunch and class periods, display preferences, a cached copy of the directory so it opens quickly, and whether you have already seen the intro animation. This information is not sent to Aperture. You can remove it at any time by clearing the site data for this page in your browser settings.

Services that load page resources

To display the pages, your browser downloads fonts, icons and code libraries from Google Fonts, Cloudflare (cdnjs), the Tailwind CSS CDN, Google (gstatic) and jsDelivr. Like any website, these providers can see your IP address and browser details when you load a page. Aperture does not control their logs; see each provider's own privacy policy.

Student information

The Student Directory shows information that a school or district already holds. Aperture does not collect, create or verify this information and does not use it for advertising or marketing. Access to student information should be limited to people the school has authorized. Questions about what is included, or requests to correct or remove a record, should go to your school office. See Schools & Districts for more.

Children's privacy

Aperture is intended to be usable by students and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through accounts, forms or tracking. Aperture has no sign-up and no advertising. If you believe information about a child was shown or stored in a way it should not have been, contact your school and Faros Technologies Inc. so it can be reviewed and removed.

Cookies and tracking

Aperture does not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers or fingerprinting. The browser storage described above is used only to make the tools work.

How long information is kept

Information saved in your browser stays until you clear it. Shared school calendar entries (dates and schedule types) remain until they are changed or removed by an authorized person.

Security

Pages are served over HTTPS where the host supports it. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so please do not enter sensitive information into Aperture.

Your choices

  • Clear your browser's site data to remove everything stored on your device.
  • Skip the optional tools that use outside services (for example, do not enter a ZIP code).
  • Ask your school to correct or remove directory information about you or your child.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the "last updated" date at the top changes with it. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Aperture is owned and operated by Faros Technologies Inc. For privacy questions or requests, contact the school staff member who shared Aperture with you, or Faros Technologies Inc. through the site where you found this link.
